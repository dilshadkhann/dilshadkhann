## Engineering Workflow

<div align="center">
<img src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=JetBrains+Mono&weight=500&size=14&duration=2200&pause=700&color=2DD4BF&center=true&vCenter=true&width=760&lines=01+Understand+the+problem+%E2%86%92+02+Design;03+Build+and+automate+%E2%86%92+04+Test;05+Monitor+and+document+%E2%86%92+06+Improve" alt="Animated engineering workflow"/>
</div>

1. **Understand** the problem, constraints, and expected outcome.
2. **Design** with security, maintainability, and operations in mind.
3. **Build and automate** repeatable steps and track changes in version control.
4. **Test and validate** rather than relying on assumptions.
5. **Monitor and document** behavior, decisions, and limitations.
6. **Improve** based on evidence, feedback, and lessons learned.
